# Current asset inventory

The Meridian includes nine Kenney Furniture Kit 2.0 models (CC0), with
project-authored placement and material overrides. See
[THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md](THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md) and the included licence and
provenance for source and file details. Most other scenery, props, materials and interface shapes are procedural;
audio is synthesized, not recorded. Eight skinned MakeHuman-derived CC0 GLBs
are present in `assets/characters/makehuman_cc0/` and are now instantiated by
`StylizedPerson` for player and NPC characters, with role-specific model choices,
textured garment tinting, skeleton-driven locomotion, and hand attachments. The
Godot runtime import and visual fit still require verification. Collision is
intentionally selective.

The Hong Kong, station and mountain environments are fictional exploration
blockouts rather than finished or licensed replicas. The public landmark is
scenic and non-destructible. Godot 4.4.1 is a separate dependency.

## Tung Chung material pass

The Tung Chung station/train now uses seven original, deterministic 256×256
material maps in `assets/procedural/`. The maps are generated by the standard-
library script `tools/generate_tung_chung_textures.py` and consumed through
`scripts/visuals/station_materials.gd`. They are original stylized albedo
patterns, not third-party scans or textures copied from a reference game. See
`assets/procedural/PROVENANCE.md` for exact filenames and regeneration steps.

## Assignment 004 city sandbox additions

The market/commercial/harbour streetscape, Vantage House tower, floor furniture,
labels, targets, collectibles, optional evidence, cyan/red Instinct overlays,
and breakable glass fragments are authored at runtime in
`scripts/world/hong_kong_annex.gd`, `scripts/world/destructible_glass.gd`, and
`scripts/missions/hong_kong_operation.gd`. They use primitive meshes,
project-authored colors/materials, and existing Kenney furniture where the
world builder references it. No new downloaded or unverified asset was added.
The city and organization are fictional; this content is not a licensed
photogrammetry/map asset or a literal real-world building. `ASSETS.md` records
provenance, not a claim of visual sign-off; the new district/tower views still
need review from executed Godot captures.

## Assignment 004 wardrobe and Ngong Ping interiors

The outfit catalogue, role badges, hats, apron, fully covering singlet/shorts,
Ngong Ping staff uniforms, landmark-interior furnishings, access-panel, ramps,
rooms, lights, signs, and NPC routes are project-authored procedural content in
`scripts/disguise/disguise_system.gd`, `scripts/visuals/stylized_person.gd`, and
`scripts/world/ngong_ping_secret_facility.gd`. They use only primitive meshes,
project-authored colors, and synthesized/project-authored effects; no new
third-party or extracted real-world Buddha asset was introduced. The outer
Tian Tan-style landmark remains a procedural, stylized, non-destructible scenic
figure. The concealed facility is fictional and is not a claim about the real
monument or its construction. This provenance statement does not substitute
for cultural review or an interactive visual/playability review.
