# Assets and dependencies

The fictional Tung Chung preview uses original primitive geometry, shared procedural materials and existing generated people; it does not contain copied station plans, external signs, models or textures. The train is a static exterior and the escalator is scenic.

## Exterior and character refinement
The Ruby scene's ground, street, buildings, sky and fixtures and the figures' facial, clothing and proportion updates are original procedural Godot geometry/materials. No external assets were imported. These are still low-poly placeholders, not textured production models.

## Current assets

The player, NPCs and Ruby use original procedural low-poly figures built from individual head, torso, limbs, clothing and accessories with simple joint motion. They remain placeholder-quality, not rigged or textured production characters. Rooms and props use original primitive geometry and materials. The Ruby sequence synthesizes simple tones; there are no recorded effects or voices. No external art or audio was imported. See [THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md](THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md). Godot 4.4.1 is a separate dependency, not bundled.

Audit tooling: `gdcorrect` 0.2.0 was run externally as an offline static checker; it is not shipped or required by the game. Its separate license is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.

## Gameplay repair additions
The assessment-unit parts and hand-attached sidearm/flash are original procedural geometry and materials, with no external models, animations or textures. These are deliberately simple, not production character assets.

## Absolute Cinema iteration
Original batched procedural dressing, character tailoring, UI geometry and synthesized gameplay sounds were created in project scripts. They have no external art/audio source. Furniture is visual-only and the low-poly figures are not professionally authored skinned models.
