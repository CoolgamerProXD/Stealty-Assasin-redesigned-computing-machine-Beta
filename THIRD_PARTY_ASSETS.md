# Third-party assets and attribution

## Kenney Furniture Kit 2.0

Nine furniture models in `assets/kenney_furniture/` are by **Kenney**, from
[Furniture Kit 2.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit), released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution is
not required by the licence, but is provided here. The original licence is
included at `assets/kenney_furniture/LICENSE.txt`, and the pinned source mirror
and complete file list are documented in `assets/kenney_furniture/PROVENANCE.md`.
The GLB files are unmodified; project code changes their placement, scale and
materials, and supplies separate collision geometry.

All other scenery, people, UI and sound effects are project-authored procedural
assets. The synthesized effects and ambient beds are not recordings. Reference
research informed design only; it did not supply copied artwork or code.

Godot 4.4.1 is a separate external tool/runtime; its licences are available at
https://godotengine.org/license/. The Windows workflow downloads official engine
and export templates and verifies against the release SHA-512 list. Audit tool
`gdcorrect` 0.2.0 was run externally and is not shipped or required by the game;
its separate licence is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.
