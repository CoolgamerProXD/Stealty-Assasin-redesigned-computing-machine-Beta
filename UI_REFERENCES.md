# HUD design references

Research consulted for functional layout and readability, not artwork or assets:

- [Hitman: Blood Money walkthrough, main-screen section](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/919985-hitman-blood-money/faqs/81248) describes separate threat/condition, contextual actions, equipment/ammunition and aiming displays.
- [Blood Money Reprisal review](https://invisioncommunity.co.uk/hitman-blood-money-reprisal-switch-review/) discusses restrained equipment visibility and minimap/alert readability.
- [World of Assassination accessibility discussion](https://www.hitmanforum.com/t/quality-of-life-suggestions-and-accessibility-options/5267) discusses minimal HUD, contextual warnings and minimap options.

PROJECT ASSASSIN uses its own layout, primitive vector equipment icon and game-provided values. There is no copied commercial art, fictional health meter, or omniscient radar. The current interface is an iteration requiring human readability and accessibility review.
